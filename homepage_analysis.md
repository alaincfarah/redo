**Page d'Accueil (Homepage) Analysis**

*   **URL de la Page**: `/fr/`
*   **Titre de la Page**: i24 Call Management Solutions - Accueil
*   **Structure des Sections**:
    1.  **Header**: Logo, language switcher, client login, and main navigation.
    2.  **Hero Banner**: Large image with the headline "Gens réels, Véritable partenariat, Résultats concrets." and a "Obtenez une soumission" CTA.
    3.  **Services de réponse d'appels**: Introduction to services with a list of service categories.
    4.  **Une communication intelligente**: Details on the benefits of their services.
    5.  **Les secteurs que nous desservons**: List of industries served.
    6.  **Partenaires**: Logos of partners.
    7.  **Comment nous mettons en place vos services**: Step-by-step onboarding process.
    8.  **Modèle de facturation progressive**: Explanation of the pricing model.
    9.  **FAQ**: Frequently asked questions.
    10. **Témoignages**: Customer testimonials.
    11. **CTA Section**: Final call-to-action for a free 30-day trial.
    12. **Footer**: Contact information, company links, service locations, and awards.
*   **Contenu Clé**:
    *   **Texte principal**: The homepage introduces i24's call management solutions, emphasizing their partnership approach and brand representation.
    *   **Appels à l'action (CTA)**: "Obtenez une soumission", "Contactez notre conseiller aux ventes pour un essai gratuit", "Appelez-nous maintenant", and "DEMANDEZ NOTRE ESSAI GRATUIT DE 30 JOURS".
    *   **Images/Médias principaux**: Images of people in professional settings, partner logos, and awards.
*   **Navigation (Liens Sortants)**:
    *   **Internal**: Links to all main pages (Services, Secteurs, À propos de nous, Balado, Blogue, Carrières, Contactez nous) and their sub-pages.
    *   **External**: Links to the client portal, social media, and a phone number.
